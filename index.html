FAQ

What's chicory and why is it in filter coffee?

Roasted chicory root adds body and a caramel bitterness that holds up to lots of milk. Our Estate Pure blend has none.

Do I need a steel filter?

No. Every blend comes with a French-press and pour-over brew guide too.

Can I skip a month?

Yes. Skip, pause or cancel from your account any time (in this concept).
